Objections, Answered

The questions every doctor asks.

"My front desk isn't technical."

Dentist OS runs where your staff already lives: WhatsApp and a browser. Confirmations and reminders send themselves. We train your front desk in one call — and we do the migration for you.

"What about patient data safety?"

Patient records are encrypted in transit and at rest, and handled in line with India's DPDP Act. Your data is yours — every record is exportable anytime, and consent is captured digitally at every step.

"I already use Practo Ray / a paper register."

Perfect. We migrate your complete patient history, treatment logs and contacts from Practo, Excel or paper cards within 24 hours — free, zero disruption. Keep the register as backup; you'll stop using it on your own.

"Will my patients actually use it?"

They don't have to adopt anything. No app downloads. Confirmations, reminders, X-rays, estimates and payment links arrive on WhatsApp — which they already check every day.

"How do multi-sitting treatments work?"

Treatment plans track milestone billings across sittings — advance, prep, cementation — with automatic UPI deposit requests before each sitting. No sitting ends in an uncollected balance.

"What about consent and legal documentation?"

Pre-built consent templates for extractions, implants and surgeries, aligned with DCI guidelines — digitally signed, stored and searchable. (We recommend your own legal review before use.)
